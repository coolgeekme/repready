# Privacy Policy — SalesReady

**Effective Date:** June 25, 2026
**Last Updated:** June 25, 2026



---

## Quick Summary

SalesReady ("the App," "we," "us") is operated by Cool Geek LLC ("the Company"). We store the minimum data needed to run the App. We do **not** sell your data, do **not** use it to train AI models, and do **not** track you across other apps.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account Information
- Email address and password (handled via Firebase Authentication; we never see your raw password)
- Display name (optional)

### 1.2 Profile & Company Information
- Information you enter: sales role, industry, target audience, company name, company website, company offerings, value propositions, brand-voice guidelines, optional uploaded brand-voice files

### 1.3 Content You Generate
- Inputs you type into AI generators (topics, prompts, custom instructions)
- Outputs returned by the AI (emails, scripts, posts, images)
- Scheduled-post metadata (date/time, platforms, optional images)

### 1.4 Social Media Connections
- When you connect LinkedIn, Facebook, or Instagram, we receive an authorization token from those platforms (via our partner **Composio**) that lets the App post on your behalf. We store the token, not your social password.

### 1.5 Technical Information
- Crash diagnostics and basic error logs (no device identifiers used for tracking)
- App version, OS version, and language preference

### 1.6 What We Do NOT Collect
- Location data
- Camera or microphone data
- Contacts
- Health, finance, or sensitive personal data
- Advertising identifiers (we do not run ads)

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data only to:
- Authenticate you and keep your account secure
- Generate AI content tailored to your role/company
- Post or schedule content to social networks at your request
- Save your past generations so you can revisit them
- Improve App stability via crash reports

We do **NOT** use your content or profile to train AI models — third-party AI providers (Anthropic, Google, OpenAI) are used under their respective enterprise terms, which prohibit using your data for training.

---

## 3. Who We Share Data With

We share data only with the **service providers required to run the App**:

| Provider | What we share | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Firebase (Google) | Email, password hash | Authentication |
| MongoDB Atlas (or equivalent) | All your saved data | Database hosting |
| Anthropic, Google, OpenAI | The text/image prompts you submit | AI generation |
| Composio | OAuth tokens, post content | Social media posting |
| Apple (App Store) | Anonymized purchase events (future paid plans) | Subscription management |

No data is sold or shared with advertisers.

---

## 4. Your Rights

- **Access**: View all data you've saved inside the App (Settings, History/Library)
- **Export**: Email team@coolgeek.me and we'll send a JSON export within 30 days
- **Delete**: Tap Settings → Sign out, then email team@coolgeek.me to permanently delete your account and all associated data within 30 days
- **Disconnect**: Remove any LinkedIn/Facebook/Instagram connection at any time from the company detail screen

Residents of California (CCPA), the EU/UK (GDPR), and other jurisdictions have additional rights including access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact us at team@coolgeek.me to exercise these rights.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

SalesReady is intended for users **17 and older** (sales professionals). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 17. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

---

## 6. Data Retention

We keep your data until you delete your account. Crash logs are retained for up to 90 days. Backups are retained for up to 30 days after deletion to allow recovery from accidents.

---

## 7. Security

We protect your data with:
- TLS encryption in transit (HTTPS for all API calls)
- Encryption at rest (provided by Firebase and MongoDB Atlas)
- Restricted server access (only authorized administrators)
- Industry-standard password hashing (Firebase Authentication)

No system is 100% secure. If we discover a breach affecting your data, we will notify you within 72 hours.

---

## 8. International Transfers

Your data may be processed in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate. By using the App, you consent to these transfers.

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be communicated in-app or by email at least 7 days before taking effect.
