## Description (4000 char max)

```
SalesReady turns the daily blank page into ready-to-send sales content.

Whether you're an SDR sending cold outbound, an AE crafting objection responses, or a founder building a personal brand on LinkedIn — SalesReady gives you AI-powered prompts tailored to your role, your industry, and your company.

● COLD EMAILS that don't sound like templates
● OBJECTION RESPONSES for the 12 most common pushbacks
● CALL SCRIPTS structured for discovery, demo, and close
● COMPANY INTEL on your prospects, fast
● RE-ENGAGEMENT scripts for stalled deals
● LINKEDIN POSTS in your voice with AI-generated images
● SOCIAL TOPIC IDEAS when you don't know what to post

Why SalesReady wins
──────────────────

✓ TAILORED TO YOU — Set your role, industry, and target audience once. Every generation pulls from your actual offering and value props, not generic templates.

✓ DROP-IN COMPANY SETUP — Paste your website and tap one button. AI reads your site and fills in your offerings, ICP, and differentiators automatically.

✓ MULTIPLE BUSINESSES, ONE APP — Manage separate company profiles with their own brand voice, social accounts, and contexts. Switch instantly.

✓ POST DIRECTLY — Connect LinkedIn, Facebook, and Instagram. Post or schedule with two taps. Multiple accounts per platform supported.

✓ SMART SCHEDULER — Quick-pick presets (Tomorrow 9am, This Fri, etc.) plus precise date/time. Schedule across platforms in one shot.

✓ GENERATE IMAGES — One tap creates an on-brand visual for your social post.

Built for:
• Sales Development Reps (SDR/BDR)
• Account Executives (AE)
• Customer Success Managers (CSM)
• Sales Engineers
• Founders building in public
• Solopreneurs running social

SalesReady is currently free. Future Pro features will require a subscription via Apple In-App Purchase.

Privacy: We store only the information you provide — your sign-in email, profile, generated content, and connected social tokens. Your content is never used to train AI models or sold to third parties.
```
