## Design tips
- Use a bold sans-serif (Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, SF Pro Display) for headlines
- Headline color: white text on a #0F172A or brand-blue (#1E40AF) background
- Phone mockup centered, headline above or below
- Keep headlines under 6 words — they need to be readable from the App Store thumbnail strip
- Match the brand-blue (#1E40AF) of your in-app primary color so the strip feels cohesive
